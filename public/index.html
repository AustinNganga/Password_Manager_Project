How to restore your checksum
    
  1. Before switching browsers or clearing browser data, open Profile on the old one and choose Copy or Download.
    2. 
     
  2. On the new browser, sign in as usual. You will see a notice that no checksum is stored there.
    3. 
     
  3. Open Profile, paste the checksum into the box above, and choose Restore.
    4. 
     
  4. Rollback protection shows as active. The next sign in is checked against it.

    
Lost it? You can still sign in. Protection starts again after your next saved change, but an older copy of your vault cannot be spotted until then.
